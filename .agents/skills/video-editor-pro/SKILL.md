# Video Editor Pro Skill

Activate this skill when structuring video timelines, pacing edits, designing audio soundscapes, writing video scripts, or selecting transitions for short-form (Reels/TikTok) or long-form (YouTube/Commercials).

## Core Editing Principles

### 1. Narrative Pacing & Cut Selection
- **The Golden Rules of Cutting**:
  - *Cut on Action*: Hide cuts in the subject's physical motion (turning head, opening door, hand gesture).
  - *Match Cut*: Match shapes, motion direction, or visual motifs across different scenes.
  - *J-Cut & L-Cut*: Split audio and video transitions for natural conversational flow (audio starts before video or carries over).
- **Rhythm Management**:
  - High Energy / Promo: Fast-paced cuts (0.5s - 1.5s per shot), beat-synced markers, dynamic speed ramps.
  - Emotional / Storytelling: Sustained takes (3s - 8s+), allowing the audience to absorb performances and atmosphere.

### 2. Audio Design & 4-Tier Layering
Always design audio in 4 distinct channels:
1. **A1 - Dialogue / Voiceover**: Clean vocal track, normalized to -3dB to -6dB peak, subtle compression & de-esser.
2. **A2 - Ambient / Room Tone**: Background noise (city rumble, cafe murmur, nature wind) to glue cuts together.
3. **A3 - Foley & SFX**: Whooshes, impact hits, foot steps, texture clicks, risers before climaxes.
4. **A4 - Music (BGM)**: Side-chain ducked (-18dB to -24dB) when dialogue is present.

### 3. Timeline / Shot List Structure
When crafting video plans, format them clearly:
```
[TIME] | [VISUAL (A-Roll / B-Roll)] | [AUDIO & SFX] | [ON-SCREEN TEXT / GRAPHIC]
```
