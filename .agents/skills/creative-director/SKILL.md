# Creative Director & Cinematographer Skill

Activate this skill whenever planning visual content, photo shoots, video productions, image generation prompts, or creative art direction.

## Core Directives

### 1. Photography & Camera Optics
- **Focal Lengths & Lenses**:
  - `16mm - 24mm`: Ultra-wide landscapes, dramatic architecture, dynamic sports perspective.
  - `35mm - 50mm`: Natural human eye perspective, documentary, street photography, environmental portraits.
  - `85mm - 135mm`: High compression, creamy bokeh ($f/1.4 - f/2.0$), flattering close-up beauty/character portraits.
  - `Macro 100mm+`: Detailed product texture, jewelry, eye reflection.
- **Exposure Triangle Mastery**:
  - Shutter Angle: $180^\circ$ rule for natural cinematic motion blur ($1/50$s for 24fps, $1/120$s for 60fps).
  - Aperture control for shallow depth-of-field ($f/1.8$) vs deep focus ($f/8 - f/11$).

### 2. Lighting Design & Schemes
- **3-Point Lighting**: Key light (45°), Soft Fill light (diffused), Rim/Hair light (separation from background).
- **Cinematic Styles**:
  - *Rembrandt Lighting*: Signature triangle light under the subject's shadowed cheek.
  - *Chiaroscuro & Low-Key*: High contrast, deep shadows, moody noir feel.
  - *High-Key*: Bright, soft, commercial, airy aesthetic.
  - *Motivated Lighting*: Practical lamps, neon signs, window light, golden hour sun flaring.

### 3. Color Grading & Look Development
- **Color Profiles**: Rec.709 vs Log (Sony S-Log3, Canon C-Log3, Blackmagic Film, Arri Log-C).
- **Color Palettes**:
  - Teal & Orange (complementary skin tone separation).
  - Monochromatic / Analogous (harmonious, moody).
  - Desaturated Bleach-Bypass / Film Emulation (Kodak Vision3 500T, Fujifilm Eterna vibes).
